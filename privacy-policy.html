Privacy Policy

Effective Date: March 2026  |  Last Updated: May 2026

Bharat Pooja Setu ("we", "our", "us") provides booking services for Pooja, Havan, and Samagri purchases. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use our mobile application.

Information We Collect

  • Authentication Data: Email address and Google Profile information (name, profile picture)
  • Phone Number: Collected for WhatsApp-based OTP verification and booking notifications
  • Location Data: Approximate and precise location to match you with nearby Pandits and Samagri vendors
  • Name and Address: Collected for service fulfillment and delivery
  • Anonymous User ID: Generated internally for session management
  • Purchase History: Booking and order records stored securely via Supabase
  • Payment Information: Processed securely via Razorpay (we do not store card details)

How We Use Information

  • To facilitate secure login and user authentication
  • To process bookings and deliver Pooja/Havan services
  • To match users with the nearest available Pandit or Samagri vendor
  • To send booking confirmations and updates via WhatsApp
  • To enable secure payment processing
  • To maintain transaction records for dispute resolution

Data Sharing with Service Providers

Your booking details (name, address, booking date/time, and ritual type) are shared with:

  • Assigned Pandit: To fulfill your Pooja/Havan service request
  • Samagri Vendor: To prepare and deliver your Samagri order

This data is shared only for service fulfillment and is not used for any other purpose.

Third-Party Services We Use

We use the following third-party services to provide app functionality. Each service has its own privacy policy:

  • Supabase: Secure cloud database and user authentication — Privacy Policy
  • Google Sign-In (Firebase Auth): For Google-based login — Privacy Policy
  • Google Maps Platform: For address search, location detection, and distance calculation — Privacy Policy
  • Razorpay: For secure payment processing — Privacy Policy
  • Meta (WhatsApp Business API): For sending booking confirmations and OTP messages via WhatsApp — Privacy Policy

Data Storage & Retention

Your data is stored securely using Supabase cloud infrastructure hosted in secure data centers. Payment processing is handled entirely by Razorpay and we do not store any card or bank details. We retain booking records for up to 2 years for legal and dispute resolution purposes.

Security

All data transmission is encrypted using industry-standard HTTPS/TLS. Access to your data is protected by Row-Level Security (RLS) ensuring users can only access their own data.

Children's Privacy

Our app is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

  • Correction: You may request correction of inaccurate data
  • Deletion: You may request deletion of your account and associated data
